Customer Display

To Connect Customer Display to this POS, please do the following:

On the Customer Display Unit
1. Open the "Customer Display" application and then go to Settings > Connect to POS unit.
2. You will see the IP address of your customer display unit on the screen. Do not close this screen while setting up your connection to the main POS unit.

On the POS Unit
1. Open Mobiz Complete Retail or Store Keeper
2. Go to Troubleshooting and Maintenance Tools > Customer Display
3. Enter the IP address displayed on on the Customer Display Unit.
4. Click on "Save" button.
